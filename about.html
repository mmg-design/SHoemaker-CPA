About

Built to do this differently.

Founder

Luke Shoemaker, CPA

Luke Shoemaker, CPA is the owner of Shoemaker CPA, one of the few CPA firms in Virginia built around planning rather than compliance. He advises mass affluent and high-net-worth individuals, closely held businesses, and their owners on complex tax and financial decisions.

Unlike traditional accounting firms that center their work around compliance, Luke takes a planning-first approach, focusing on the gray areas where the tax code and financial planning intersect, and where structure and strategy drive outcomes. Rather than treating tax work as a commodity, he works with clients to develop integrated strategies that connect tax planning with broader financial decisions.

He works with a limited number of clients who are seeking a long-term advisory relationship and who value insight, clarity, and thoughtful decision-making over transactional services like tax preparation.
